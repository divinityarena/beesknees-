🍪 Cookie Policy

Last updated: June 2026  ·  GoibniuDevs, Belfast

The short version: We use a small number of cookies and local storage items to make the app work — saving your votes, remembering dismissed ads, and keeping you signed in. We don't use advertising cookies or sell your data.

What are cookies?

Cookies are small text files stored on your device when you visit a website. We also use localStorage — a similar browser feature that stores data locally on your device. Both make the app remember things so you don't have to.

What we store and why

Third-party services

  • Google Places API — powers our search results. Calls are made server-side so Google does not set cookies in your browser directly. Google Privacy Policy →
  • Supabase — handles user accounts and vote storage. Supabase Privacy Policy →
  • Booking.com — hotel search links include an affiliate parameter. If you click through and book, Booking.com may set cookies on their site. Booking.com Privacy Policy →
  • Formspree — handles contact and feedback forms. Formspree Privacy Policy →

What we don't do

  • We do not use advertising or tracking cookies
  • We do not sell or share your data with third parties for marketing
  • We do not use third-party analytics that track you across sites
  • We do not build profiles for targeted advertising

How to manage cookies

You can clear localStorage and cookies at any time through your browser settings. In most browsers: Settings → Privacy → Clear browsing data.

Clearing these will sign you out of your account and reset your vote history on this device. Your votes remain in our database if you were signed in.

Your rights (GDPR)

If you're based in the EU or UK, you have the right to:

  • Access the data we hold about you
  • Request deletion of your account and associated data
  • Object to processing of your personal data
  • Lodge a complaint with your national data protection authority (ICO in the UK, DPC in Ireland)

To exercise any of these rights, use the feedback form on the main app or email hello@beesknees.best.
